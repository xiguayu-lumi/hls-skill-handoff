# Paper Analysis Framework

## Required Output

`paper-analysis.md` records:

- bibliographic identity and missing metadata
- one-sentence research question
- paper type
- core contribution
- studies, samples, measures, comparisons, and time points
- main, null, mixed, moderation, mediation, and limitation findings
- causal scope and generalization boundary

## Classification

### Method framework

The contribution is a conceptual or procedural framework. Emphasize problem, method position, stages, common mistakes, checklist, and limits.

### Tool workflow

The contribution is a reproducible tool or pipeline. Emphasize gap, workflow, operations, template, comparison, risk, and human oversight.

### Empirical study

The contribution comes from collected data. Separate each study, design, sample, measure, effect, null result, mechanism, and limitation.

### Systematic review or meta-analysis

The contribution synthesizes studies. Emphasize search, inclusion, distribution, overall effect, heterogeneity, subgroup evidence, quality, and practical boundary.

## Causal Decision

- Randomized experiment: causal wording only for manipulated exposure, measured outcome, study sample, and reported time window.
- Quasi-experiment: qualified causal wording with design limitations.
- Observational, cross-sectional, experience sampling: association wording.
- Longitudinal: temporal ordering is not automatically causality.
- Mediation: evidence for an indirect statistical path is not proof of a complete mechanism.
- Moderation: a conditional pattern is not a universal subgroup law.
