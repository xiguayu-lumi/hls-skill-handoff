# Visual System

## Principles

1. Evidence first: charts and diagrams show reported evidence or clearly labeled structure.
2. Light canvas: white/near-white dominates; cobalt creates hierarchy.
3. Controlled orange: only risk, action, or decisive contrast.
4. Asymmetric rhythm: alternate dense evidence pages with spacious synthesis pages.
5. Angular identity: cut corners and forward edges echo the HLS Logo.

## Forbidden Patterns

- repeating full-width dark-blue headers
- blue-white-red thesis-report styling
- dense paragraphs with many red bold fragments
- generic gray tables pasted full-page
- decorative gradients, 3D icons, cartoons, or unrelated AI art
- excessive rounded SaaS cards

## Chart Rules

- Main study or AI group: cobalt.
- Neutral control: gray.
- Secondary group: sky blue.
- Risk, negative boundary, or key discrepancy: orange.
- Show units, sample size, and time point.
- Do not truncate axes to exaggerate differences.
- Do not draw unreported values.

## Footer

Every factual content page includes an Evidence Footer with evidence IDs and source page/figure/table. The footer is readable but subordinate to the main conclusion.
