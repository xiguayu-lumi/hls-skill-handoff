# Research and evidence

## Source priority

1. School course profile or official syllabus.
2. Official assessment detail page and course handbook.
3. Official school/department pages for names, logos and campus context.
4. User-provided files.
5. Third-party pages only as discovery leads; do not cite them as course rules when official evidence exists.

## Minimum fact sheet

Record before writing:

- course code and exact English title;
- school, faculty or coordinating unit;
- semester/year, campus, mode and units/credits;
- prerequisites, assumed knowledge and incompatible courses if shown;
- learning topics or outcomes;
- every assessment name, weight and due period;
- late submission, extension and hurdle rules;
- final/core assessment mode, duration, location/security conditions and permitted materials;
- official URL and screenshot filename for each fact group.

## Evidence screenshots

- Keep browser chrome or enough page context to identify the source.
- Capture a full source screenshot first, then create a cropped excerpt for layout.
- Do not crop away the assessment name, weight, date or section heading that gives a number meaning.
- Use filenames such as `01-course-overview.png`, `02-assessment-summary.png`, `03-core-assessment-details.png`.

## Fact language

- `官方信息｜` introduces direct course facts.
- `HLS 解读：` introduces editorial interpretation.
- `学习建议：` introduces an action based on the facts.
- When a date or rule may update, say “以最新 Course Profile 为准”.
