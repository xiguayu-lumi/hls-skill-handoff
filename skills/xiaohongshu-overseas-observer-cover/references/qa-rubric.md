# QA Rubric

Score each item 0 or 1. A final cover should score at least 24/26 and must pass all starred items.

This rubric applies to the fully composed cover. A pure illustration panel cannot pass final QA and must be labeled as an intermediate asset.

## Deliverable

- [ ] * The reviewed primary output is a complete 3:4 magazine cover, not a standalone illustration.
- [ ] * Masthead, bilingual editorial text, right journal rail, rules, and bottom illustration are all present.

## Geometry

- [ ] * Canvas is 3:4.
- [ ] * Right rail stays within its column.
- [ ] * Illustration begins at the specified horizontal boundary.
- [ ] * No illustrated object crosses the boundary.
- [ ] Main text has at least 24 px left safe margin.

## Typography

- [ ] * Chinese glyphs render correctly.
- [ ] * Main Chinese headline uses a visibly heavy serif weight and dominates the editorial hierarchy.
- [ ] * Main Chinese headline has no orphan character.
- [ ] English headline has consistent capitalization.
- [ ] Summaries have comfortable line length.
- [ ] Summary lines end before the Journal divider with deliberate right-side white space.
- [ ] Masthead remains the secondary visual anchor, not larger than the Chinese story hook in perceived weight.

## Content

- [ ] Chinese and English meanings are aligned.
- [ ] Summary explains rather than repeats the headline.
- [ ] No unsupported factual claims.
- [ ] No price, contact, or tutoring claim unless requested.

## Illustration

- [ ] * Style is vintage storybook, not anime or photoreal.
- [ ] * No random text, letters, numbers, logo, signature, watermark, or malformed object.
- [ ] Character and props are centered and visually balanced.
- [ ] Panel color supports rather than competes with the title.
- [ ] The scene does not copy a supplied character, costume, pose, or composition.

## Series quality

- [ ] Recognizable as Overseas Observer.
- [ ] Clearly original rather than a direct copy.
- [ ] When reviewed as a series, the cast and prop logic are not repeated mechanically.
