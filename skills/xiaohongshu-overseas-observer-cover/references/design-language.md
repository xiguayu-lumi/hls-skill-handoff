# Design Language — Overseas Observer

## Editorial character

The cover combines three visual traditions:

1. British and American newspaper mastheads
2. literary-journal article layouts
3. vintage European children’s-book illustration

The intended feeling is thoughtful, collectible, calm, and slightly nostalgic. It should not look like a tutoring advertisement or a generic AI poster.

## Composition

- Warm paper upper field with a narrow right-side journal rail.
- Strong left alignment and large serif type.
- The Chinese headline is the primary hook: use a heavy Song/Ming weight and an oversized scale.
- The English text supplies editorial atmosphere and context.
- Keep the summary text block narrower than the left column so it ends well before the Journal divider and preserves editorial white space.
- The bottom colored panel supplies warmth and emotional storytelling.
- The boundary between paper and illustration must remain perfectly clean.

## Distinctive series markers

Keep these stable across posts:

- `THE OVERSEAS OBSERVER`
- globe motif
- year and journal rail
- four numbered categories
- warm paper background
- oversized Chinese Song/Ming headline
- full-width bottom illustration panel

Allow these to vary:

- line breaks
- English headline position
- short decorative rules
- illustration panel color
- illustration character and props
- right-rail copy if the post category changes

## Anti-copy adjustments

Do not recreate another cover pixel-for-pixel. At least three of the following should differ from the supplied reference:

- masthead size or line split
- globe placement
- English headline width
- Chinese line breaks
- summary separator treatment
- rail number spacing
- illustration color
- illustration subject
