Compare and contrast two fast-food restaurants in terms of their target audience and marketing strategies.

When it comes to fast food, there are two names that come to mind almost immediately: McDonald's and Burger King have been competing for the same customers for decades, each with distinct approaches to the same market.[1] Both restaurants offer similar menus, but their marketing strategies and target audiences differ significantly.

说到快餐，几乎立刻想到两个名字：麦当劳和汉堡王数十年来一直在争夺同样的顾客，各自以截然不同的方式进入同一个市场。两家餐厅提供类似的菜单，但它们的营销策略和目标受众有显著差异。

McDonald's might dominate with its cheap yet satisfying breakfast, but Burger King rules over the lunch crowd.[2] This fundamental difference in peak hours reflects each restaurant's broader strategy.

麦当劳可能在便宜又令人满意的早餐上占据优势，但汉堡王统治着午餐时段。这种高峰时段的根本差异反映了每家餐厅更广泛的策略。
[1]
The opening effectively establishes the comparison. Consider tightening "have been competing" to a more active verb.
开头有效地建立了对比关系。考虑将"have been competing"收紧为更具主动性的动词。
[2]
This is a good compare/contrast thesis statement. It signals the essay's direction clearly.
这是一个很好的比较 / 对比型 thesis statement。它清晰地标示了文章方向。

McDonald's targets families with children through its Happy Meal toys and play areas. The company's mascot, Ronald McDonald, was specifically designed to appeal to young children and create brand loyalty from an early age.[3]

麦当劳通过开心乐园餐玩具和游乐区来瞄准有孩子的家庭。公司的吉祥物麦当劳叔叔专门设计来吸引幼儿，并从早期就培养品牌忠诚度。
[3]
The specific example of Ronald McDonald makes the claim about targeting children more concrete and convincing.
麦当劳叔叔的具体例子使"瞄准儿童"这一论点更加具体和有说服力。
